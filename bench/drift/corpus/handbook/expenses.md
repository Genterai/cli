# Expenses

Spend company money the way you would spend your own if you were careful with it. This page covers company cards,
reimbursements, allowances and what needs approval first. Booking trips is covered in [Travel](travel.md).

## Company cards

Everyone who regularly buys things for work gets a virtual card in **Pleo**. Your card has a monthly limit set by your
manager, usually between €300 and €1,000.

- Take a photo of the receipt in the Pleo app right after paying. Missing receipts are chased every Friday.
- Add a short note on what the purchase was for and pick the right category.
- Software subscriptions go through #it-help first, so we do not end up with five accounts for the same tool.
- Personal purchases on a company card by mistake: tell Finance and they will deduct it from your next salary.

## Reimbursements

If you paid with your own money, submit the receipt in the expenses app as an out-of-pocket expense within 30 days.
Reimbursements are paid with the next salary run.

## Needs approval first

| What | Who approves |
| --- | --- |
| Any single purchase over €500 | Your manager, before you buy |
| Hardware not on the standard list | IT and your manager |
| Conferences and courses | Your manager (comes out of your learning budget) |
| Anything for a customer (gifts, dinners) | Sales lead or CEO |

## Allowances

### Home office

New joiners get a one-time budget of **€750** to set up their home office: a desk, a chair, a monitor, a headset. Keep
the receipts; the furniture is yours after two years. We also pay up to €25 a month towards your internet connection.

### Learning

Everyone has a learning budget of €1,000 per calendar year for books, courses and conferences. Unused budget does not
carry over.

### Per diem on business trips

For every day you travel for work you get a flat daily allowance of **€48** for food and small costs, no receipts needed.
On days where the company already pays for lunch and dinner (the offsite, a conference with catering), the allowance is
halved. Claim it in the expenses app with the trip dates.

### Phone

If you are in an on-call rotation we pay €30 a month towards your phone plan. Claim it once per quarter.

## Mileage

Using your own car for work: €0.23 per kilometre. Commuting to the office does not count.

## What we do not pay for

- Fines (parking, speeding)
- Alcohol, except at team events with the organizer's approval
- Upgrades to business class without approval (see [Travel](travel.md))
- Lost-luggage costs that the airline or insurance should cover

## Questions

Ask in #finance or email finance@northwindlabs.io. Finance closes the books on the fifth working day of each month;
expenses submitted after that land in the next month's run.
