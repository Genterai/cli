# Time off

Rest is part of the job. This page covers vacation, public holidays, sick leave and other leave. Everything is requested
and tracked in **Personio**; your manager gets a notification and approves it there.

## Vacation

Full-time employees get **27 days** of paid vacation per calendar year, on top of public holidays. Part-time colleagues
get the same number pro rata (for example, someone working four days a week gets 21.6 days, rounded up to 22).

- The year runs from January 1 to December 31. If you join mid-year, your allowance is pro rata from your start date.
- Up to five unused days carry over to the next year. Carried-over days expire on March 31.
- Take at least one block of two consecutive weeks per year. Your brain needs it, and so does the bus factor.
- Contractors through an employer of record follow the leave rules of their local contract.

### Requesting vacation

1. Check the team calendar so not everyone in your team is away at once.
2. Request the days in Personio at least two weeks ahead for anything longer than three days.
3. Once approved, add an out-of-office event to your calendar and set a Slack status.
4. If you are in an on-call rotation, swap your shifts first (see the on-call pages in `ops/`).

## Public holidays

You get the public holidays of the country you are employed in. If you work on a public holiday because of on-call or a
release, you get a day off in lieu within the following month. People Ops publishes the list for each country in
January.

## Sick leave

If you are sick, stay home and get better. Tell your manager (a Slack message is enough) before your usual start time if
you can, and mark the day in Personio.

- You do not need a doctor's note for up to **three consecutive days**.
- From the fourth day, we need a note, or whatever your country's rules require instead.
- Sick days never come out of your vacation allowance.
- If you get sick during your vacation, those days become sick days; tell People Ops when you are back.

## Other leave

| Kind | Allowance | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Moving house | 1 day | Once per year |
| Wedding or registered partnership | 2 days | Your own |
| Bereavement | up to 5 days | Close family; talk to your manager if you need more |
| Caring for a sick family member | up to 4 days per year | Beyond that, we find a solution together |
| Volunteering | 1 day per year | For a registered charity |
| Parental leave | statutory | A company-wide policy is being drafted by People Ops |

## Unpaid leave and sabbaticals

After two years at Northwind you can ask for up to three months of unpaid leave. Talk to your manager and People Ops at
least three months ahead so we can plan cover.
