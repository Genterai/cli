# Business travel

Travel is occasional at Northwind: the yearly offsite, some customer visits and a few conferences. This page covers how
to book and what the company pays for. Spending while you are away (cards, receipts, the daily allowance) is in
[Expenses](expenses.md).

## Before you book

1. Get your manager's approval in writing (a Slack message is fine) for anything involving a flight or more than one
   night away.
2. For conferences, post in #learning so others can join, or ask you to bring back notes.
3. Check the team calendar so a whole team is not away in the same week.

## Booking

All flights, trains and hotels are booked through **TravelPerk**, so that we know where everyone is if something goes
wrong and the invoices reach Finance automatically. Bookings made elsewhere are reimbursed only if TravelPerk was more
expensive or had no availability; attach a screenshot as proof.

### Flights

- Economy is the default for every flight.
- Premium economy is allowed for flights **longer than 6 hours** (a single leg, gate to gate).
- Business class needs written approval from the CEO and is very rarely granted.
- Book at least 14 days ahead when you can. Last-minute fares are often twice the price.
- One checked bag per trip is covered. Seat selection is covered for flights over two hours.

### Trains

Prefer the train for trips under five hours door to door. First class is allowed on trains when the trip takes more than
three hours, or when it is cheaper than second class (it happens).

### Hotels

| City tier | Nightly cap | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tier 1 | €220 | London, Paris, New York, San Francisco, Zurich |
| Tier 2 | €160 | Berlin, Amsterdam, Austin, Lisbon, Warsaw |
| Tier 3 | €120 | Everywhere else |

If the conference hotel is above the cap, you may book it anyway when it saves a commute; mention it in the booking note.

## Ground transport

- Public transport first. Taxis and ride-hailing are fine late at night, with luggage, or when it saves real time.
- Rental cars only when there is no reasonable alternative. Take the basic insurance; our travel insurance covers the
  rest.

## Insurance and safety

Our travel insurance covers every employee on a business trip, including medical costs abroad and cancellations. The
policy number and the emergency phone number are in the 1Password item "Travel insurance".

If something goes wrong while you are away, call the emergency number first, then tell your manager. TravelPerk's
support line can rebook flights around the clock.

## After the trip

Submit any receipts you have not uploaded yet within 30 days, and claim the daily allowance for your travel days.
