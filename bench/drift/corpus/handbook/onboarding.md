# Onboarding

Your first weeks at Northwind should feel calm. Nobody expects you to ship anything big in your first month. This page
explains what happens before you start, in your first week, and after.

## Before your first day

- People Ops sends your contract and a welcome email with your start time (usually 10:00 local time).
- IT orders your laptop so that it arrives at least two working days before you start.
- Your manager writes a first-month plan and shares it with you on day one.
- You get an onboarding buddy; you will meet them in your first week.

## Your laptop

Engineers get a **MacBook Pro 14-inch with the M3 Pro chip**, 36 GB of memory and 1 TB of storage. Everyone else gets a
MacBook Air 13-inch with 16 GB. Laptops are enrolled in our device management before they ship, so you sign in with your
Google account on first boot and the security settings are applied automatically.

- You can ask for an external keyboard, mouse and one monitor through #it-help; bigger setups come out of your home
  office budget (see [Expenses](expenses.md)).
- Laptops are replaced every three years, or sooner if they break.
- If you leave, the laptop goes back to IT. We send you a prepaid box.

## First day

1. Sign in to your Google account and set up a hardware security key (IT sends one with the laptop).
2. Join Slack. Start with #general, #people-ops, #it-help and your team channel.
3. Get access to GitHub, Linear, 1Password and Personio. IT has already invited you; accept the invites.
4. Engineers: request AWS access through the SSO portal and read [Access control](../security/access-control.md).
5. Have lunch (on us) with your buddy.

## First week

- Read [Welcome](welcome.md) and skim the rest of the handbook.
- Engineers: set up your machine with [Local development](../engineering/local-dev.md) and get a small change merged.
  Your buddy will find you a starter issue.
- Non-engineers: shadow a support shift with the Support team. It is the fastest way to learn how customers use Beacon.
- Meet your manager for your first one-on-one and go through the first-month plan.

## Buddy program

Every new joiner gets a buddy from another team. Your buddy is there for the questions you would rather not ask your
manager: where things are, who knows what, how decisions really get made.

- The pairing lasts for your **first six weeks**. You meet at least once a week for half an hour.
- After that you can keep meeting, of course; most pairs do.
- Buddies get a small budget for a coffee or lunch together, which they claim as a team expense.

## First month

| Week | Goal |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Accounts, laptop, first merged change or first support shift |
| 2 | Understand how Beacon runs a check end to end (see [Architecture](../engineering/architecture.md)) |
| 3 | Own a small piece of work from start to finish |
| 4 | Thirty-day check-in with your manager and People Ops |

## On-call

Engineers join an on-call rotation after three months, never earlier. Before your first shift you shadow two shifts and
do a game day with the Platform team.

## Feedback

At the end of your first month we send a short survey about your onboarding. Please be honest; we change this page
based on the answers.
