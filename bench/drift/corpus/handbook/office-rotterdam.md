# Rotterdam office

Our only physical office is a rented floor in a shared building on the Maashaven, a ten-minute walk from Rotterdam Zuid
station. It has twelve desks, two call booths and a meeting room for eight. Nobody has an assigned desk.

## Address and access

- Address: Maashaven Noordzijde 140, 3rd floor, 3072 AE Rotterdam
- Building hours: 07:00–21:00 on weekdays. The building is closed at weekends.
- Your access badge is handed out by the office manager, Ruben de Graaf, during your first visit.
- Lost badge: tell Ruben the same day so it can be blocked. A new one costs €15, which we pay the first time.

## Booking a desk

Desks are booked in the "Rotterdam desks" calendar in Google Calendar, one event per desk per day. Book before 18:00 the
day before so the cleaners know how many people to expect. The meeting room has its own calendar and is bookable in
30-minute slots.

## Wi-Fi

| Network | Who | How to connect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NWL-Staff | Employees | Sign in with your Google account (certificate-based, set up by device management) |
| NWL-Haven-5G | Guests | Password is on the card at the reception desk and in 1Password ("Office guest Wi-Fi") |

Do not connect personal devices to NWL-Staff. Use the guest network for phones and anything not managed by IT.

## Lunch and kitchen

- On Tuesdays we order lunch for everyone in the office. Sign up in #rotterdam-office before 10:00.
- Coffee, tea and fruit are free. Please run the dishwasher before you leave if it is full.
- The fridge is cleared every Friday afternoon.

## Parking and bikes

There is no car parking for us in the building. The public garage on the Brede Hilledijk offers a reduced day rate when
you show the office badge. Bikes go in the basement storage; the door uses the same badge.

## Visitors

Guests sign in at the building reception and are picked up there. If you invite a candidate or a customer, tell Ruben
the day before so they are expected.

## Emergencies

The building has its own emergency team. When the alarm sounds, leave by the nearest stairs and gather on the quay in
front of the building. First-aid kits are in the kitchen and next to the meeting room.
