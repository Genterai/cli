# Sales playbook

How we sell Beacon: who we sell to, how a deal moves, and the rules for pricing and contracts. Most customers sign up and
buy on their own; Sales works with the ones that want an invoice, a contract or a conversation.

## Who we sell to

- **Ideal customer**: a software company with 50 to 2,000 employees, a platform or SRE team, and customers who notice
  when they are down.
- **Buyer**: usually the head of infrastructure or platform. Finance gets involved above $10,000 per year.
- **Not a fit**: agencies that want to resell monitoring under their own brand, and companies that need on-premises
  installation.

## Pipeline

All deals live in **HubSpot**. Every deal has an owner, an amount, a close date and a next step; deals without a next
step are flagged in the Monday pipeline review.

| Stage | Exit criteria |
| --- | --- |
| Qualified | We know the problem, the buyer and the timeline |
| Demo done | The buyer has seen Beacon on their own targets |
| Trial | A trial workspace is set up with success criteria agreed in writing |
| Proposal | Pricing and terms sent |
| Negotiation | Redlines or procurement in progress |
| Closed won / lost | Signed, or a reason for losing is recorded |

## Demos

Demo on the prospect's own endpoints whenever you can; a demo on our sample targets converts half as well. Prepare by
creating a workspace with three of their public URLs already monitored. Bring a Solutions Engineer for anything
involving private probe locations or SSO.

## Trials

Sales-assisted trials last 30 days and must have written success criteria (for example, "alerts reach PagerDuty within
one minute of an outage"). Check in after one week and two weeks.

## Discounts

Discounts are a tool, not a habit. Always trade them for something: a longer term, upfront payment, a case study.

| Discount on list price | Who approves |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 10% | The account executive |
| 10% to 20% | Head of Sales (Daniel Okafor) |
| Above 20%, or any free months | Ilse Brandt, our CFO, in writing |

Multi-year deals can get up to 10% extra per additional year, which counts towards the limits above.

## Contracts

- Standard terms are on the website; most customers accept them as they are.
- Redlines on liability, data processing or security go to Legal through the #legal-requests channel.
- Security questionnaires go to the Security team; the answers for the common ones are in the trust center.

## Renewals

The account owner starts the renewal conversation **90 days before** the contract ends. Review usage first: customers
who use less than half of what they pay for are a churn risk, and a smaller plan they keep is better than a big plan
they cancel.

- Send the renewal quote at least 45 days before the end date.
- Price increases at renewal need the notice described in [Pricing](../product/pricing.md).
- Auto-renewing contracts still get a check-in call.

## Handover to Support

When a deal closes, the account executive writes a short handover note in the customer's HubSpot record: who the
contacts are, what they bought, what they care about, and any promises made during the sale. Support reads it before
the kickoff call.
