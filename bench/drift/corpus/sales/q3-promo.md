# Summer annual-plan promotion

From July 1 until **September 30, 2026**, new customers who choose yearly billing get 20% off their first year on the
Team or Business plan. The goal is to move more self-serve customers to yearly billing before the autumn price review.

## The offer

- 20% off the first year of a yearly Team or Business subscription.
- Only for new subscriptions, and for existing monthly customers switching to yearly.
- Not combinable with non-profit, education or other discounts.
- After the first year, the subscription renews at the normal yearly price.

## Coupon code

Customers enter **BEACONSUMMER26** at checkout. The code is created in Stripe as a coupon with a 12-month duration and is
limited to the Team and Business yearly prices, so it cannot be applied to anything else by accident.

Sales can apply it to a quote manually in the billing admin panel. Support can apply it after the fact for up to seven
days after a customer subscribed without it.

## Where it is promoted

| Channel | What | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| In-app banner | Shown to monthly Team and Business customers | Web team |
| Pricing page | Badge on the yearly toggle | Marketing |
| Newsletter | July and September editions | Marketing |
| Trial emails | Day 10 and day 13 of the trial | Marketing |
| Sales | Mentioned in proposals for yearly deals | Account executives |

## Messaging

Lead with the yearly saving, not the discount: "Pay yearly and save almost three months this year." Avoid language that
sounds like a fire sale; we are not in trouble, we are rewarding commitment.

## Tracking

The campaign dashboard in Metabase shows coupon redemptions per plan and per week, and how many monthly customers
switched. The target is 150 redemptions over the three months.

## FAQ

**Can a customer on the Starter plan use it?** No. Starter is excluded because its yearly price is already low.

**What if a customer upgrades from Team to Business during the discounted year?** The discount carries over to the new
plan for the rest of the first year.

**Can we extend it for a big deal that closes in October?** Ask the Head of Sales. The default answer is no.

## After the campaign

Marketing removes the banner and the pricing badge on October 1, and Revenue engineering expires the coupon in Stripe the
same day. Results are presented in the October revenue meeting.
